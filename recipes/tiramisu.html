Tiramisù

Tiramisù is one of Italy’s most beloved desserts. Originating from Veneto, it’s a rich, creamy layered dessert made with coffee-soaked ladyfingers, mascarpone cream, and cocoa powder. Its name means “pick me up,” thanks to the energizing mix of coffee and sugar.

Ingredients

  • 300 g ladyfingers (savoiardi)
  • 500 g mascarpone cheese
  • 4 eggs, separated
  • 100 g sugar
  • 300 ml strong espresso coffee, cooled
  • Unsweetened cocoa powder (for dusting)
  • Dark chocolate shavings (optional)

Directions

  1. Prepare the espresso coffee and let it cool.
  2. Whisk egg yolks with sugar until pale and creamy. Add mascarpone and mix until smooth.
  3. In a separate bowl, beat egg whites until stiff peaks form, then gently fold into the mascarpone mixture.
  4. Dip each ladyfinger quickly in the coffee (don’t soak too much) and arrange a first layer in a dish.
  5. Spread half of the mascarpone cream over the ladyfingers. Repeat with another layer of soaked ladyfingers and the remaining cream.
  6. Dust generously with cocoa powder (and add chocolate shavings if desired).
  7. Chill in the fridge for at least 4 hours before serving.
