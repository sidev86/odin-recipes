Polenta Taragna

Polenta taragna is a traditional dish from Northern Italy (Valtellina and Lombardy). It’s a rustic, hearty polenta made with a mix of cornmeal and buckwheat flour, enriched at the end with butter and local melting cheeses, giving it a rich, creamy, and slightly nutty flavor.

Ingredients

  • 250 g buckwheat flour
  • 250 g cornmeal (polenta flour)
  • 200 g butter
  • 250 g soft, melting cheeses (e.g. Bitto, Casera, Taleggio)
  • Coarse salt to taste

Directions

  1. Bring the salted water to a boil in a large pot.
  2. Gradually pour in the mixed flours while stirring constantly with a wooden spoon to prevent lumps.
  3. Cook over medium-low heat for 40–50 minutes, stirring often.
  4. When cooked, stir in the butter and diced cheeses until the polenta becomes smooth, creamy, and stringy.
  5. Serve hot.
